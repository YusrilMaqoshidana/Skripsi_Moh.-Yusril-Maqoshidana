\frontmatterchapter{ABSTRACT}

% --- ISI ABSTRACT ---
\noindent High message activity in WhatsApp groups frequently causes information overload, making it difficult for members to track discussion dynamics and extract key insights. While topic modeling can automate thematic extraction, the standard BERTopic framework relying on HDBSCAN inherently discards up to 42\%--56\% of messages as outliers, causing critical context loss on short and informal conversational texts. This research aims to develop an enhanced BERTopic architecture that integrates the IndoBERTweet contextual embedding model, BIRCH hierarchical clustering, and the BM25 weighting scheme to minimize outlier rates while improving topic coherence and diversity. The methodology involves processing a corpus of 51,584 cleaned WhatsApp messages through a BIRCH clustering pipeline featuring dynamic micro-cluster thresholding and automatic topic reduction. Contextual representations generated by IndoBERTweet are dimensionally reduced via UMAP before being structured into cohesive thematic sub-clusters. The model evaluation across a multi-stage learning curve demonstrated outstanding performance, successfully restricting the outlier rate to just 7.21\% (retaining 92.79\% data coverage) while achieving a positive topic coherence ($C_{\text{NPMI}}$) of +0.1075 and a topic diversity of 0.9875, significantly outperforming HDBSCAN-based baselines. Furthermore, the comparative analysis proved that hierarchical clustering effectively resolves topic fragmentation on short texts without sacrificing semantic quality. The study concludes that adapting hierarchical clustering within the BERTopic pipeline serves as an effective mechanism to capture scattered conversational context. The resulting model was successfully integrated into an interactive web application prototype featuring real-time SSE progress streaming and message context traceability, enabling users to efficiently explore group discussion dynamics.
