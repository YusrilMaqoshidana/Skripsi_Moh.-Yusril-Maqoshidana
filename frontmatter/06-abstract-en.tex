\frontmatterchapter{ABSTRACT}

% --- ISI ABSTRACT ---
\noindent High message activity in large-scale WhatsApp groups frequently causes information overload, making it difficult for users to track discussion dynamics and extract key insights without manually reading thousands of messages. Meanwhile, the standard BERTopic topic modeling framework relying on HDBSCAN inherently discards $40.44\%$--$57.36\%$ of messages as outliers, eliminating conversational continuity on short and informal community texts. This research aims to develop an enhanced BERTopic architecture that integrates the IndoBERTweet contextual language model, BIRCH hierarchical clustering with micro-threshold optimization, the BM25 weighting scheme enriched with a nine-category WhatsApp discourse stopword dictionary, and an automatic topic reduction pipeline. The methodology involves modeling a corpus of 51,583 cleaned messages preprocessed from the WhatsApp group chat history of the Book Community. Empirical evaluation demonstrates that the proposed model successfully restricts the outlier rate to $6.83\%$ (retaining $93.17\%$ real data coverage or $48,060$ messages), significantly outperforming baseline retention. On topic quality evaluation, the proposed model achieves the highest Topic Diversity of $0.9653$, a solid positive Topic Coherence ($C_{\text{NPMI}}$) of $+0.0495$, a robust Intra-Topic Similarity of $0.6129$, and an Embedding Density of $0.5419$. This architecture was successfully implemented into a web-based analytics system (\textit{ChatAnalisis}) featuring an asynchronous FastAPI backend and real-time SSE progress streaming, where functional black-box testing across 14 core MVP test cases achieved a $100\%$ valid pass rate.
